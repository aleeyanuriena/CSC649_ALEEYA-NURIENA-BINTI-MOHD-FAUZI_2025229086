1.1	Project Background
UiTM uFuture is an online learning platform used by Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) students to access course materials, assignments, assessments, and class attendance. Throughout a semester, students need to manage academic activities across several courses. However, important information such as assignment deadlines and attendance records is accessed through individual course pages, requiring students to navigate between different sections.
To address this issue, this project proposes a UI/UX enhancement of the existing UiTM uFuture platform. The proposed enhancement focuses on introducing a centralized Academic Dashboard and improving three existing pages: My Courses, Assignment, and Attendance. These improvements aim to provide clearer information, easier navigation, and a more organized interface to help students manage their academic activities efficiently.
