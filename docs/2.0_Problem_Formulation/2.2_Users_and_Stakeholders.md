# 2.2 Users and Stakeholders (A2)

The primary users for the proposed UI/UX enhancement are UiTM students that use uFuture platform to manage their academic activities. The proposed interface is designed to help students check assignment deadlines, identify the important tasks and monitor their attendance more easily.
The main stakeholders are UiTM students, lecturers and uFuture administrators. UiTM students are the direct users of the proposed interface. Lecturers and administrators are indirectly involved as they are part of the existing academic platform
