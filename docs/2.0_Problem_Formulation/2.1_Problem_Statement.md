# 2.1 Problem Statement (A1)

UiTM students may face difficulty in managing their assignment, deadline and class attendance on the existing uFuture platform. Students are required to open different course pages to check their academic tasks and attendance information. Thus, it is difficult to access all information in one place.

Based on personal observation, sometimes students forget to mark their attendance on uFuture. They need to contact their lecturer after missing the attendance check-in. Besides, it is difficult for students to identify which assignment needs to be addressed first as the deadline is displayed separately in different courses.

Thus, students may miss the important academic activities, take more time to navigate to different course pages and face difficulty in managing their academic activities efficiently.
