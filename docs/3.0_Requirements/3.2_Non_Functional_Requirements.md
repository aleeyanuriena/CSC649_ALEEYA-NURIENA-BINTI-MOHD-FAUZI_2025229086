
## 3.2 Non-Functional Requirements (B2)

The non-functional requirements describe the quality standards of the proposed UiTM uFuture enhancement, including usability, accuracy, reliability, and consistency.

| ID | Category | Non-Functional Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| NFR1 | Usability | Students shall access assignment deadlines within three clicks from the dashboard. |
| NFR2 | Usability | Students shall access the Attendance page within two clicks from the dashboard. |
| NFR3 | Accuracy | Priority labels shall follow the defined IF–THEN rules for each assignment status and deadline. |
| NFR4 | Reliability | All main navigation paths in the prototype shall work without broken links. |
| NFR5 | Consistency | All four proposed pages shall use a consistent navigation layout. |
